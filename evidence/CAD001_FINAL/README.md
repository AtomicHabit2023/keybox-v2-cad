# CAD-001 Final Baseline Evidence

This package records the restored, correct CAD-001 master-door baseline for final design review.

## Controlling interpretation

- The complete formed door envelope is **87.5 x 12.0 x 47.0 mm**.
- The uninterrupted planar front region is a derived result of approximately **84.8 x 41.6 mm**.
- The formed envelope fits the **90 x 50 mm** cell pitch with nominal gaps of 2.5 mm horizontally and 3.0 mm vertically.
- All four 90-degree bends are healthy and point in the required inward/hidden-return directions.
- The sheet-metal body and flat pattern are healthy.

## Files

- `CAD001_FINAL_front.png` - front review view of the restored baseline.
- `CAD001_FINAL_top.png` - top review view of the restored baseline.
- `CAD001_FINAL_isometric.png` - isometric review view of the restored baseline.
- `CAD001_FINAL_MEASUREMENTS.json` - machine-readable geometry, feature-health, fit, and scope checks.
- `KeyBox_V2_CAD001_MasterDoor_FINAL.f3d` - saved native Fusion checkpoint of the restored baseline.

## Provenance and scope

The images, measurements, and native checkpoint are taken from the saved original CAD-001 baseline that predates and supersedes the abandoned oversized CAD-001R attempt. No CAD-001R artifact is included in this package. No Fusion geometry was modified while preparing this final review package, and CAD-002 was not started.

This checkpoint is for design review only and is not a released manufacturing blank.
